# Privacy Policy: Universal Creator & Freelancer Data Exporter

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

## Summary
This extension runs locally in your browser. It does not collect, sell, or share your personal data, and it has no analytics or tracking.

## What the extension accesses
- **Page content (only when you click the extension icon):** the extension reads tables on the currently active tab so it can preview them and export them to a CSV file. This data is processed on your device and is never sent to any server.
- **Exported files:** CSV files are created on your device and saved through your browser's download feature.

## What is stored
- **License key and activation status:** if you activate Pro, your Gumroad license key, the email address linked to the purchase, and the last verification time are stored locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). You can remove them at any time with the "Deactivate" button or by uninstalling the extension.

## Third-party services
- **Gumroad (api.gumroad.com):** when you activate Pro, and periodically afterward, the extension sends your license key to Gumroad to confirm it is valid. No page content or table data is sent. Gumroad's handling of this information is described in its own privacy policy: https://gumroad.com/privacy

## Data sharing
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties, other than the license check with Gumroad described above.
